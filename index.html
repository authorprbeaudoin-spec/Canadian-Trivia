🍁 Canadian Trivia Quiz 🍁

Test your knowledge of Canada's geography, history, culture, and sports!

🗺️ Geography (15 Questions)
1. What is the capital of Canada?
Ottawa
2. Which is Canada's largest province by area?
Quebec
3. What are the five Great Lakes?
Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, and Ontario
4. Which mountain range includes Mount Robson?
The Rocky Mountains
5. What is Canada's easternmost province?
Newfoundland and Labrador
6. Which river is the longest in Canada?
The Mackenzie River
7. How many provinces and territories does Canada have?
13 (10 provinces and 3 territories)
8. Which is Canada's smallest province by population?
Prince Edward Island
9. What is the capital of British Columbia?
Victoria
10. Which Canadian city is known as the "Gateway to the West"?
Winnipeg
11. What is the capital of Alberta?
Edmonton
12. Which Canadian waterfalls are among the most famous in the world?
Niagara Falls
13. What is the capital of Saskatchewan?
Regina
14. Which national park is Canada's oldest?
Banff National Park
15. What is Canada's third-largest city?
Vancouver
📚 History (15 Questions)
1. In what year did Canada achieve Confederation?
1867
2. Who was Canada's first Prime Minister?
Sir John A. Macdonald
3. What document established Canadian Confederation in 1867?
The British North America Act
4. In what year did Canada gain complete independence?
1982 (with the patriation of the Constitution)
5. Who was the first Indigenous Prime Minister of Canada?
There has not been one yet
6. What year was the Canadian flag officially adopted?
1965
7. Which explorer is credited with discovering Canada?
John Cabot (1497)
8. What was the name of the fur trading company that explored Canada?
The Hudson's Bay Company
9. In what year did Canada declare independence from Britain?
Gradually from 1867 onward (1982 for full patriation)
10. Who was the first European to reach the Pacific coast from Canada?
Alexander Mackenzie (1793)
11. What was the War of 1812?
A conflict between the United States and the British Empire (fought in Canada)
12. Who led the Quiet Revolution in Quebec?
Jean Lesage and later Pierre Trudeau
13. What year did Canada become a full nation within the Commonwealth?
1926 (Balfour Declaration)
14. Which Canadian general is famous for defending Canada in the War of 1812?
General Isaac Brock
15. What was the purpose of the transcontinental railway built in Canada?
To connect eastern and western Canada and encourage settlement

1. What is Canada's national food sport?
Hockey
2. What is poutine?
French fries topped with gravy and cheese curds (Quebec specialty)
3. How many official languages does Canada have?
Two: English and French
4. Who is the famous Canadian magician and illusionist?
David Copperfield
5. What Indigenous peoples are native to Canada?
First Nations, Métis, and Inuit peoples
6. What is a traditional Canadian winter sport played on ice?
Curling
7. Which Canadian singer is known for songs like "Closer"?
The Weeknd
8. What is Canada's national symbol animal?
The beaver
9. Who is the famous Canadian actor from Deadpool?
Ryan Reynolds
10. What is Maple syrup primarily made from?
Sugar maple tree sap
11. Who is the Canadian author famous for the Anne of Green Gables series?
Lucy Maud Montgomery
12. What Canadian film festival is held annually in Toronto?
Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF)
13. What is Tourtière?
A traditional Canadian meat pie, especially popular at Christmas
14. Which Canadian musician won a Grammy for "Bad Guy"?
Justin Bieber
15. What is the national holiday celebrated on July 1st?
Canada Day

1. Which Canadian ice hockey team won the most Stanley Cups?
Montreal Canadiens (24 Stanley Cups)
2. Who is the Canadian hockey legend known as "The Great One"?
Wayne Gretzky
3. In what year did Canada first win the Winter Olympics hockey gold?
1952 (men's) and 2002 (women's)
4. Which city hosted the Winter Olympics in 2010?
Vancouver
5. What is the name of Canada's national ice hockey league?
National Hockey League (NHL)
6. Who is the Canadian tennis player known for winning Wimbledon?
Milos Raonic or Eugenie Bouchard
7. Which Canadian football team won the most Grey Cups?
Toronto Argonauts and Winnipeg Blue Bombers (tied)
8. Who is the famous Canadian figure skater known for landing the first triple axel?
Axel Paulsen (Norwegian, but the jump is named after him)
9. In what year did the Toronto Blue Jays win the World Series?
1992 and 1993
10. Which Canadian Olympic athlete is known for winning gold in speed skating?
Catriona Le May Doan or Clara Hughes
11. What sport did Terry Fox run to raise awareness for?
Marathon running (to raise cancer awareness)
12. In which sport did Canada host the Summer Olympics?
Montreal (1976)
13. Which hockey player is known as "The Rocket"?
Maurice Richard
14. What is the Canadian Football League's championship called?
The Grey Cup
15. Which Canadian golfer won the Masters Tournament?
Mike Weir (2003)
